# Task Spec — Operational Shadowing

Executed by `fde-assessor` in Phase 1. It is the fieldwork that underpins the quality of both the blueprint and the estimated ROI.

## Objective

Observe the client's real process being performed, collecting quantitative data (time, volume, error) and qualitative data (decision points, exceptions, pain).

## Preconditions

- NDA signed.
- Fit score approved (Phase 0 GO or partial GO).

## Steps

1. Schedule at least 3 one-hour sessions with **real operators** performing the task — do not substitute an interview with a manager.
2. In each session, record:
   - Start and end time of every run observed.
   - Average daily, weekly and monthly volume (ask, and confirm against a client report where possible).
   - Points where the operator has to consult another system or person.
   - Decision points that require human judgement rather than a fixed rule.
   - Error and rework frequency, observed or reported.
3. Identify the unstructured documents and data involved (PDFs, emails, spreadsheets, audio) — this feeds the Phase 2 pipeline design directly.
4. After the sessions, validate the collected numbers with the manager or sponsor (this avoids reporting an operator's perception as confirmed data).
5. Consolidate the data into a form ready for the `blueprint.md` and `roi-calculation.md` templates.

## Output

- Shadowing notes (free text is fine, but must contain the key numbers: average time, monthly volume, error rate).
- List of the systems and documents involved.

## Antipatterns

- Replacing real shadowing with a questionnaire sent by email — it loses the implicit decision points that only direct observation reveals.
- Accepting a monthly volume from memory without trying to confirm it in the client's reports or systems.
